\# **POS Agent Revenue Insights Dashboard**



Power BI dashboard analyzing revenue, profitability, transaction outcomes,

agent performance, location performance and monthly trends across a

synthetic POS transaction dataset.

\## Business Problem



A POS agent network needs visibility into revenue performance, transaction

outcomes, agent contribution, location performance and changes in revenue

over time.



The objective was to build an interactive management dashboard that brings

these indicators together and highlights areas requiring further investigation.



\## Dataset



\- Period: January–June 2026

\- Transactions: 5,000

\- Dataset type: Synthetic

\- Dimensions: Agents, locations, transaction types and transaction outcomes

\- No real customer or agent information is included.

\## Key Insights



\- Approximately ₦714K in revenue was generated across 5,000 transactions.

\- Reported profit was ₦526.62K.

\- Transaction success rate was 95.73%.

\- Cash Withdrawal was the largest revenue contributor.

\- AG008 generated approximately ₦101K, the highest agent revenue shown.

\- Karu generated approximately ₦101K, the highest location revenue shown.

\- Revenue recovered from a February low to a May high before declining

&#x20; approximately 11% in June.



\## Recommendations



1\. Investigate the drivers of the June revenue decline.

2\. Examine high-performing agent activity for potentially repeatable practices.

3\. Investigate lower-performing locations for opportunities to increase activity.

4\. Explore ways to diversify revenue beyond Cash Withdrawal.



\## Analytical Limitations



\- The dataset is synthetic.

\- The analysis covers six months.

\- The June decline does not establish causation.

\- Additional analysis would be required to identify the specific drivers

&#x20; behind the decline.
